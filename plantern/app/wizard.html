Measure a room

Tap the wall on the map that you share with the room next door (or pick it here). Corner 0 goes on the far side of that wall, and your first wall runs back along it, so the two rooms end up sharing one wall.
At the next corner, does the wall continue…
Angle inside the room at the corner: 90 = square, 135 = bevel, 60 = sharp, over 180 = inside corner.
The length of that last wall is worked out for you. If you measured it anyway, type it above and it is used as a check.
