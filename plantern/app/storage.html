Settings

Where your houses are kept: a folder on this computer, a network share or a git repository. Plantern keeps them up to date and notices changes made elsewhere.

Home Assistant

The Home Assistant your lamps and switches live in. Plantern connects to it itself: the access token stays in Plantern's settings on this computer and is never sent to a page.

Placed objects

Objects made from blueprints keep their 3D file in Plantern's settings folder on this computer, not in the house. Clearing them frees the disk; they are built again when they are needed.
