Materials & colours
Walls

Walls follow their rooms: applying a room moves its walls and fills the edges nothing covers (one-room walls get the default outer thickness, marked as assumed and drawn hatched).

Floor areas

Net floor area per room: the outline less what stands on the floor (chimney, pillar) and the stairwell. A room without a floor has none; balconies and terraces are counted apart.

Anchors

The same physical point on two levels (a stairwell corner, an outer corner): the Editor shows whether the levels line up and can move a level to make them.

Checks

Control measurements: two points and what the tape or laser says, the furthest off first. Measure two points (📏, point – point) and Keep as check. A check between two corners of a room measured by lengths and diagonals can join its solve (in the solve).

Measurements

A round pillar: its diameter, and where it stands measured like a ceiling lamp, from two walls of the room it is in: at right angles from each wall's face to the pillar's surface. Without a room, type its middle. It moves with those walls when the room is measured again.

Walk clockwise, room on your right. Corner 0 is where you start; wall A runs from corner 0 to 1, B from 1 to 2, and so on (the letters are on the plan). Lengths are listed in that order. All lengths in mm.

Place next to another room…

Click the neighbour's wall on the plan (or pick it here), then the wall of this room that goes against it. This room is moved (and turned if needed, never mirrored) so the two share one wall with the thickness you enter.
